# Audio

All files here are placeholders generated procedurally by a small Python script (sine tones and filtered noise), made for this project. No third-party or AI-generated audio. Replace them with real recordings when ready.

- `sfx/footstep_*.wav`: low-passed noise bursts.
- `sfx/interact.wav`: two rising sine blips (interaction confirm).
- `sfx/touch.wav`: short low-passed noise tap with a low thump (hand touching something).
- `music/placeholder_loop.wav`: 8 s quiet sine-chord loop (C, Am, F, G).
- `narrator/placeholder_desk_intro.wav`: ~3 s of speech-like beeps for the example narrator cue.
