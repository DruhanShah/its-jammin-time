# Ending: blurry vision → spectacles → real-life video → credits

User request: after the lights come back the third time you walk back to your office; the view keeps flickering between blurry and normal; a pair of spectacles is on your desk; the narrator tells you to try them on; once on, a video recorded in real life plays (placeholder for now); then the credits roll and the game ends.

## Plan
1. Story: `Story.Step.FREE_ROAM` replaced by `ENDING` (the final step; free roam had no content left). Objective = the spectacles on the player's desk in Start (off-path nags point there).
2. Vision flicker: full-screen `hint_screen_texture` disc blur (Compatibility/WebGL2-safe, no mipmaps), slow irregular eased spells.
3. Spectacles prop with an Interactable, spawned only in ENDING (no edit to office.tscn).
4. Putting them on: glasses slide down over the eyes (sharp through the lenses), narrator line, then a full-screen Theora video.
5. Credits roll in comic panels, THE END, narrator sign-off, end screen with Quit (desktop only).

## As built
- `core/story.gd`: `Step.ENDING` replaces `FREE_ROAM`; arrival cue `ending_lights_back`; `Story.REAL_LIFE` = `res://ending/real_life.tscn`. F7 in ENDING jumps to the video. `narration/to_be_continued.tres` deleted (unused).
- `world/office/story_stage.gd` (ENDING only): adds a `VisionBlur` and instances `world/office/props/spectacles.tscn` into `Rooms/Start/Furniture` at `SPECTACLES_SPOT` (left of the keyboard). Blur strength `outside_blur_strength` 0.4 outside Start, 1.0 inside. First time in Start: `ending_glasses_hint`; then `ending_glasses_nudge_1/2` every `glasses_nudge_time` 20 s in Start. Disables `DeskNarratorTrigger` so "Ah, the desk..." can't talk over it. `objective()` returns the spectacles. If the power comes back while the office is loaded (an in-world restore game), `step_changed` sets the ending up and plays the arrival cue once the narrator is quiet. Interacting: hides the prop, freezes the player, hides the HUD, plays `ending_glasses_on`, `VisionBlur.put_on()`, waits for the line (max 6 s), then `Transition.change_scene(Story.REAL_LIFE)`.
- `world/office/vision_blur.gd` + `.gdshader` (`class_name VisionBlur`, CanvasLayer 0: above 3D, below HUD/subtitles): 24-tap golden-angle disc blur of the screen texture, radius 1.4 % of screen height (resolution-independent). Rhythm: blurry spells 0.9–2.6 s (drift in over 0.3–0.8 s), sharp spells 1–3 s (×3 at low strength; a third are short 0.6–0.9 s "almost" moments), snapping sharp in ~0.3 s. No flashes, no instant cuts. Hidden (zero cost) while sharp. `put_on()`: blur to max, then a comic pair of lenses (rounded-box SDF, ink frame, bridge, glint) drops from the top with a back-ease; sharp inside, blurry + darker outside. Verified in the Compatibility renderer (screenshots).
- Spectacles: `assets/models/spectacles/glasses_ipoly3d.glb`, "Glasses" by iPoly3D (Poly Pizza, CC0), credited in its CREDITS.md and game/CREDITS.md. The glb's RootNode is ×100 (FBX cm), so the Model node is scaled 0.095 (~21 cm, a little bigger than life so it reads on the desk). `spectacles.gd` recolours the frame red and the lenses translucent blue (the model's black frame disappeared on the black desk). Interactable verb "try on the glasses" (prompt key from the InputMap, so it follows the ESDF/C shift).
- `ending/real_life.tscn/.gd`: black background, `AspectRatioContainer` (ratio from the video texture) + `VideoStreamPlayer` (expand). Hides the mouse, stops music/narrator, Esc skips after 1 s, on `finished` → credits.
- Placeholder video `assets/video/real_life.ogv`: 1280×720, 30 fps, 8.2 s, ~300 KB, Theora + Vorbis (a tick each second, to check audio). Made with Godot 4.7's Movie Maker, which writes .ogv directly: `Godot --path game --resolution 1280x720 --write-movie <abs path>.ogv --fixed-fps 30 res://<scene>.tscn` (+ an `override.cfg` with `editor/movie_writer/video_quality=0.35` to keep it small; `--quit-after` dropped frames, quitting from the scene after N process frames worked). ffmpeg on this machine is broken, not needed.
- **Swapping in the real video:** replace `game/assets/video/real_life.ogv` with the same filename (or point `Video.stream` in `ending/real_life.tscn` elsewhere). Format: Ogg Theora video + Vorbis audio (.ogv), the only format Godot plays natively, also on the web (software-decoded: keep it ≤ 1280×720, 24–30 fps, a modest bitrate, e.g. `ffmpeg -i in.mp4 -c:v libtheora -q:v 6 -c:a libvorbis -q:a 4 -vf scale=1280:-2 real_life.ogv` on a working ffmpeg, or export to Ogg Theora from Shotcut/Kdenlive). Any aspect ratio works (letterboxed). Audio plays on the Master bus.
- `ending/credits.tscn/.gd`: built in code. Halftone blue sky, comic panels (paper, 5 px ink border, solid offset shadow, yellow caption tab): "MEANWHILE, IN THE CREDITS...", A GAME BY (the 5 team names from the plan Appendix, no handles/emails), 3D MODELS, FONTS (+ Comic Sans / trademark notes), SOUNDS, ENGINE, SAFETY NOTICE gag, then THE END (stops at 40 % height, pops), `credits_end` line, then "Thanks for playing!" + Quit (desktop) / "You can close this tab now." (web, no Quit). Hold Space/Enter/mouse to fast-forward ×6. Quit resets the comic cursor first (avoids exit-time texture leaks). The list is the `SECTIONS` const: keep it in step with game/CREDITS.md (assets added on other branches, e.g. gargoyle/quiz sounds, must be added there after merging).
- No "Play again" (GameState has no reset).
